# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, as a maintainer, contributor or
user, should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body,
ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect creators' rights; do not use this project's spaces to share or
  request copyrighted material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing or requesting pirated content, account credentials or ways to get
  around access controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who do so
repeatedly or seriously.

Report problems privately to
[izeno.contact@gmail.com](mailto:izeno.contact@gmail.com). Reports are kept
confidential.
